HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. Full pipeline.

01

Crawl

Point it at any careers page. It auto-detects the board, paginates through every listing, and pulls each job's full details — title, location, requirements, description.

📥 Auto-pagination · Detail extraction
02

Match

Your resume is read and semantically compared against every JD. AI scores each job across skills, experience, location preference, and seniority.

🤖 AI semantic · 0-100 score · Per-dimension breakdown
03

Report

A clean PDF handbook is generated — ranked by match score, grouped by city, with apply links and personalized tips for each position.

📄 PDF/HTML · Clickable apply links · Priority labels
